### Your task

Write an essay on the importance of learning to evaluate information critically in the age of artificial intelligence. Explain your view with reasons and a concrete example.

**建议时间**：审题与列提纲3分钟 → 写作22分钟 → 检查5分钟。时间细分为学习建议。

### 写前思考

1. 中心论点：为什么“能生成内容”不等于“内容可信”？
2. 具体例子：一个学生怎样核验 AI 给出的参考文献？
3. 行动建议：追溯来源、交叉核对、承认不确定性。

> [!success]- 写完再看：参考范文
> As artificial intelligence becomes part of everyday study, the ability to evaluate information critically is increasingly important. Convenient access to an answer should not be confused with reliable access to knowledge.
>
> One reason is that fluent language can make unsupported claims appear convincing. A student, for example, may receive a clear explanation accompanied by references that do not actually exist. If the student copies the answer without checking, a useful learning tool becomes a source of error. Critical evaluation requires asking where a claim comes from, whether independent evidence supports it, and what uncertainty remains. These questions are valuable even when the information comes from a teacher, a website or a textbook rather than a machine.
>
> Universities should therefore teach verification as a practical habit. Students can compare sources, follow citations and explain why they trust particular evidence. AI can still help them generate questions and organise ideas, but responsibility for the final judgement remains human. In this way, technology supports learning without replacing the careful thinking on which learning depends.
